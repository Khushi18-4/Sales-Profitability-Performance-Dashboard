# 📈 Maincraft Technology Internship Project: Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains the end-to-end Sales & Profitability Business Intelligence project completed during my internship at **Maincraft Technology**. The objective was to analyze transactional sales data to find critical trends regarding regional sales, top product categories, high discounting impacts, and seasonal variations.

## ⚙️ Tech Stack & Workflow
* **Excel:** Initial data cleaning, structural normalization, and removing anomalies.
* **MySQL:** Database schema setup and writing complex aggregation queries to fetch performance metrics.
* **Power BI:** Building interactive executive dashboard visualizations, cross-filtering, and timeline slicers.

## 💡 Key Business Answers
* **Highest Sales Region:** West Region emerges as the absolute market leader in revenue generation.
* **Most Profitable Category:** Technology category delivers the maximum net margins, while Furniture shows high sales but thin margins due to logistics.
* **Discount Impact:** Promotional discounts exceeding **20%** drastically turn profitable orders into net financial losses.
* **Best Month:** **December** marks the absolute peak for annual revenue driven by holiday and festive shopping.

## 🎯 Executive Recommendations
1. Enforce a mandatory management approval flow for discounts exceeding 20%.
2. Allocate a higher inventory buffer and marketing capital to the Technology segment.
3. Optimize shipping costs and restructure pricing metrics for the Furniture segment.
